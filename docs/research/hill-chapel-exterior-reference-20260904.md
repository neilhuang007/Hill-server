# Hill Chapel exterior references and modeling constraints

Verified 2026-09-04. Scope narrowed to the Alumni Chapel and its immediate grounds. This is reference research, not an in-game or Google Street View acceptance result. Photographs, architect drawings, and selected frames from the school's 2026 campus video were visually inspected.

## Current geometry and orientation

The Chapel is location **6** on the current official campus map, west of the main Quadrangle. Athey Academic Center is **3**, Dining Hall / Millhiser Family Dining Room **4**, John P. Ryan Library **2**, Hunt Upper School Dormitory **29**, and Shirley Quadrivium Center **50**. The Chapel's east side faces the Quadrangle; tennis courts are west; private East King Street runs past its south end. The current school page is [Our Campus](https://www.thehill.org/about/our-campus), with this [2026 labeled map PDF](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1787600757/thehillorg/fky5ch2qhgl6lcsvhz06/FY26CampusMap_Labeled.pdf). Earlier `/about-us/.../campus-map` URLs returned 404 during this investigation.

**The 2023-24 expansion is on the south side.** Langan's December 2022 application explicitly proposes a 1,445 sq ft (~134 m²) southern addition plus curbs, walks, retaining walls, lights, and planting. The school confirms work began June 2023 and the expanded Chapel was rededicated September 27, 2024. Thus 2021 orthophotos and older footprints cannot establish its present southern boundary. Sources: [Langan submission, page 3](https://www.pottstown.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/13647?fileID=6169), [school Chapel history](https://www.thehill.org/student-life/spiritual-life).

GKO Architects publishes the proposed site plan, footprint plan, side elevation, and sections on its [Hill project page](https://www.gkoarchitects.com/planning/lansdowne-borough-mp-fs-ns5h3). The addition spans the west aisle, central nave, and east ambulatory. It is a short transverse block, not a long narrow nave extension. Measuring the raster site plan's 0-80 ft bar gives approximately **19.6 m width and 7.7 m depth**, with entry steps/landing extending another approximately 4-5 m. Method: the 2104×1517 image's 80 ft scale spans ~278 pixels; addition width ~224 pixels and depth ~87.5 pixels. Allow approximately ±1 m. These are **approximate proposed dimensions**, not final construction dimensions. The old projecting vestibule overlaps this envelope; the nominal 134 m² added area should not be forced into a width×length rectangle. Align the extension with the old south nave wall rather than the old porch tip. Source: [GKO proposed site plan](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/646f78c6b1f0ec681875e979/1698239704225-R29XIPHWNJ5IB44H8P0H/HILL%252BSCHOOL%252BRENDERS%252B2%252BHR.jpg).

## Exterior features to preserve

The school's [2025 front photograph](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/f_auto,q_auto/v1752866412/thehillorg/bmtum5s58dvh26p6q2tl/2025-Alumni-Chapel.jpg) shows warm brown/gray stone, tan surrounds, the south gable and cross, a tall pointed-arch window above wooden double doors, projecting buttresses, smaller upper windows, entrance steps, black metal rails, and green copper lanterns. Use this photograph to judge the completed south front; older images show a different projecting entrance porch. It is a ground-level oblique photograph, not a measured elevation.

The restoration contractor identifies **brownstone veneer and dimensional stone, stone window tracery, and copper flashings**. Its 2020 account says asphalt shingles had replaced the original slate roof. That is a dated material record; verify any later replacement visually. The contractor photographs show the east-facing square clock/bell tower, higher polygonal corner turret, parapet openings, paired belfry louvers, slender slit windows, repeated buttresses, upper arched windows, and the open pointed-arch ambulatory facing the Quad. Sources: [Witmer project record and photographs](https://thewitmergroup.com/project/the-hill-school-alumni-chapel/), [whole east-side view](https://thewitmergroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/The-hill-school-hero.png), [arcade detail](https://thewitmergroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/SIDE-SHOT-2.png).

The school's [2026 campus fly-through](https://resources.finalsite.net/videos/t_video_mp4_480/v1782935559/thehillorg/knkakkud0xzfegdmwlq9/2026-Drone-Fly-Through-Full-Video.mp4) provides additional current context: approximately **108-112 seconds** approaches the tower from the Quad, **133-136 seconds** exits the east ambulatory toward the lawn, and **137-140 seconds** shows the Quadrangle overhead. These views help confirm orientation and silhouette; the overhead segment alone cannot verify a facade.

## Material and window assessment against native v4

The material target is **cut brownstone masonry**. [Witmer](https://thewitmergroup.com/project/the-hill-school-alumni-chapel/) explicitly identifies brownstone veneer and dimensional features; the current school photograph shows irregular rectangular stones with pale mortar. Brownstone is a variety of sandstone ([Getty material definition](https://www.getty.edu/vow/AATFullDisplay?find=tow&logic=AND&note=&subjectid=300011403)). A matching stone material should take priority over a mud-brick color approximation.

The installed `26.1.2.jar` at `C:/Users/neil_/AppData/Roaming/.minecraft/versions/26.1.2/` was inspected directly. Its vanilla textures do not provide a close brownstone match: `stone_bricks` has appropriate stone masonry but is cool gray; `red_sandstone` is strongly orange; ordinary sandstone is pale yellow; granite is speckled pink; `bricks` and `mud_bricks` depict different masonry. The inspected jar has no `red_stone_bricks` blockstate. The preferred proposal is **`stone_bricks` with an original local brownstone resource-pack texture**, retaining the stone-brick stair/slab/wall family. Their model JSON files all reference `minecraft:block/stone_bricks`, so one texture override keeps those forms consistent. The jar's `version.json` reports resource format **84.0**.

For vanilla trim, use `smooth_sandstone` and its stair/slab forms for dressed openings, `sandstone_wall` for narrow tracery posts, and `cut_sandstone` sparingly for larger dressed corner stones. These are proposals based on the observed warm tan trim; the vanilla yellow tone still needs comparison under game lighting. Use the brownstone stone-brick stair/slab family along the gable and aisle coping where a strong pale outline would exaggerate the photograph's more continuous masonry. Neither red nether bricks nor fantasy materials are appropriate substitutions.

Comparison with native v4's `south.png` shows the large front window as two fields divided by one thick bar. The [current front photograph](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/f_auto,q_auto/v1752866412/thehillorg/bmtum5s58dvh26p6q2tl/2025-Alumni-Chapel.jpg) instead clearly has **three lower glazed lights, two slender mullions, and branching pointed tracery above**. Proposed starting dimensions are a 2.6-2.8 m clear width, approximately 4.3-4.55 m height, a 0.30-0.35 m frame face, and glass recessed 0.25-0.4 m behind the exterior wall. These are rough interpretations of an oblique photograph, not measured dimensions. The v4 generator's frame volume extends 0.55 m outward from the wall plane, making the trim visibly bulky; move the frame closer to the wall face while retaining a recessed pane layer and a continuous pointed arch.

Texture scale also needs native verification: at two blocks per metre, a texture containing nine stone courses and repeated once per block makes each course only about 5.6 cm high. Start near two or three courses per block, then compare with the photograph. Texture resolution alone does not change this physical pattern scale. Extracted vanilla swatches and the magnified window reference are saved under `runtime/research/hill-reference-20260904/vanilla-26.1.2-material-audit/`. The reviewed native captures are under `runtime/campus-reconstruction/chapel-native-qa/runs/v4-smoke-20260904T153729/screenshots/`; their inspection is not visual acceptance.

## Interior observations and reusable building components

**Observed Hill features, not a measured interior survey:** the school's [2026 fly-through](https://resources.finalsite.net/videos/t_video_mp4_480/v1782935559/thehillorg/knkakkud0xzfegdmwlq9/2026-Drone-Fly-Through-Full-Video.mp4), approximately 116-129 seconds, shows warm brown wooden pews in two main banks separated by a center aisle, shaped timber ends and backs, additional side seating, and the southern balcony. Frames `chapel-drone-22.jpg`, `-24.jpg`, `-32.jpg`, `-33.jpg`, and `-35.jpg` were inspected. No distinct upholstery or colored cushion strips are discernible; do not infer red or blue cushions from generic church builds. The footage also shows a polished brown aisle, pale warm brick walls, dark exposed timber roof framing, pendant lights, and multicolored stained glass. Frame `-28.jpg` (~122 s) shows lighter wooden individual chairs around a plain dark wooden table near the northern end. Exact pew counts, spacing, wood species, and cushion presence remain unmeasured.

**Component proposals, separate from Hill evidence:** the user permits panes, stairs, slabs, trapdoors, carpets/cushions, and pressure plates wherever their appearance fits the real object. The five references below provide techniques; their palettes and complete buildings are not Hill references.

| Primary or creator-owned reference | Technique and proposed Hill use |
| --- | --- |
| [Mojang: Taking Inventory — Glass Pane](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/taking-inventory--glass-pane) | Mojang describes panes as a way to add depth to windows. Recess connected panes within the masonry opening; retain narrow mullions and the pointed head. Choose stained-glass colors from a particular Hill window rather than treating the entire opening as opaque black glass. |
| [dai: Gothic Window Design tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IjcmhfdON_A) (2025) | The creator's preview shows a tall window with a layered pointed frame and slender divisions. Adapt the framing principle using compatible masonry stairs, slabs, and wall pieces around the pane plane. The preview was inspected; the full video sequence was not played. Preserve Hill's window proportions and tracery instead of importing this window unchanged. |
| [Camisha / ChromaGlitch: Minecraft Gable Roof Guide](https://chromaglitch.com/2025/01/24/minecraft-gable-roof-guide/) | The illustrated guide combines upright and inverted stairs with slabs. Use dark roofing variants for smoother slopes and eaves, and masonry variants to soften arch shoulders. For Hill's lower aisle roofs, use the same pieces along a single outward slope; the generic tutorial's complete gable shape does not describe those aisles. |
| [Minecraft Furniture: Slab and Trapdoor Chair](https://minecraftfurniture.net/furniture/chairs-stools/slab-and-trapdoor-chair/) | This creator example uses slabs, trapdoors, and carpet. Extend the wooden seat/back principle into pew rows, with thin end panels. Start with a warm oak/spruce palette and bare wooden seats to match the current visible evidence. A carpet cushion remains an available variant if a clearer Hill photograph supports one; the tutorial's red fabric is not a Hill color reference. |
| [Minecraft Furniture: The Fence Hatch Table](https://minecraftfurniture.net/furniture/table/the-fence-hatch-table/) | The creator demonstrates fence legs with pressure-plate tops. This is a useful small-table component. For the wider plain Chapel table visible in frame `-28.jpg`, a continuous wooden slab top and separate legs are the closer proposed adaptation. |

Apply these details at the study's **two blocks per metre** scale rather than copying generic furniture dimensions blindly. Keep pane connections, stair orientation, slab halves, carpet support, and walking clearances in the native-game audit. These are component recommendations, not a change to the Chapel-only scope or an acceptance result. ## Proposed acceptance checks for the Chapel trial

1. Match the south-front photograph and east tower/arcade photograph with separate eye-level game cameras; also inspect one elevated view for roof and footprint alignment.
2. Judge the silhouette first: tower dominates the nave; turret is higher; aisles are lower; front cross and gable remain recognizable; south addition has the observed full width.
3. Preserve open arcade arches and recessed glass/doors. Generic repeated rectangular windows and a solid arcade wall fail this check.
4. Select blocks by exterior role from a small construction palette. Brownstone should read as coherent warm masonry, roofs as coherent dark roofing, and trim as intentional bands. Never sample ores, sculk, luminous minerals, or unrelated decorative blocks because their average RGB happens to match a shaded texture.
5. Let game lighting create shadows. Do not bake black texture patches into permanent wall materials. Keep glass limited to windows and lanterns limited to their fixtures.
6. Check the approach on foot: connected steps and landings, usable paths, terrain meeting foundations, and no floating floors, buried doors, abrupt flat platform, or tree fragments crossing the walls.
7. Keep all uncertain dimensions in the generation manifest. A visually plausible inferred detail must not be labeled measured or street-view verified.
