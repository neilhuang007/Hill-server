The current review package is `runtime/campus-reconstruction/foster-v14-2x`. Its
`world/`, `sample-blocks.npz`, `manifest.json`, and six-camera
`camera-views.json` are generated by `scripts/hill_foster_details.py` using
`server-assets/hill-foster-reference.json`. All six V2 and all six V3 native

window trim and V3's faint sash surrounds were rejected. The white-pane V4

rejected opaque cap bands inside the glazing. V14 moves those supports
outside the glazed height and restores transparent inner returns. All six
v14 native views are inspected and accepted for bounded integration;
remaining visual approximations and source limits are explicit in its

or photographic evidence.

## Building-specific original vanilla materials

The architect-authored [2013 campus master plan](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/hilll-school-master-plan-2013/58408750)
explicitly distinguishes Foster and Rolfe's red brick from the darker masonry
of the historical quad. Ordinary `bricks` preserve that documented material
and its bond pattern. The north facade's video pixels are heavily blue-shaded;
they are not used as an intrinsic brick reflectance measurement.

The profile contains three reproducible ROI samples with hashes, image pixel
bounds, linear-light means, CIELAB values, and CIEDE2000 comparisons against the
85-candidate original-vanilla catalogue. The roof ROI is `[522,123,607,145]` in
`foster-rolfe-pitch-238s-crop.png`, and its mean is RGB
`[127.04,125.31,126.11]`. `stone_bricks` score ΔE00 **0.916**; dark
`deepslate_tiles` score **24.690**. Stone brick stair/slab geometry therefore
remains the better available gray overlapping-unit proxy. Slate versus a
replacement shingle is not independently established.

White painted trim is represented by quartz blocks, slabs, and stairs, without
claiming that the original trim substrate is quartz or limestone. Pale oak
doors are real operable vanilla doors; their unmodified texture is a pale
timber approximation of the photographed blue-gray paint. The shared role
allowlist was extended by the integration agent to permit this ordinary door.
No resource pack, sandstone, ores, sculk, or blanket campus color substitution
is used. The source client is vanilla 26.1.2, SHA256
`b1b3158572666445eff01e82fad8c7de2e4953db6d354f311730d77a8359d0b0`.
