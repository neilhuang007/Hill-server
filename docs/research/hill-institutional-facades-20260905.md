# Hill institutional facades and actual material evidence — 5 September 2026

The material inventory comes first. The previous Minecraft palette suggestions are withdrawn pending the root agent's texture and color comparison. A pale stone-looking block is not evidence that the real building is sandstone. Quadrivium's documented surrounds are **limestone**, its walls are **brick**, and its historic roofs are **slate**. The architect describes salvaging limestone surrounds and matching new brick to the existing brick's color and texture. The contractor and structural engineer independently corroborate these materials. [SMP project account](https://smparchitects.com/construction-progress-the-shirley-quadrivium-center-at-the-hill-school/), [Wohlsen project account](https://wohlsenconstruction.com/project/the-hill-school-shirley-quadrivium-center/), [CVM structural report](https://www.dvase.org/docs/awards2021/3/03%20-%20CVM%20Shirley%20Quadrivium%20Center_hill_school.pdf).

The structured register is [institutional-materials.json](../../runtime/research/campus-full-detail-20260905/institutional-materials.json). It separates documented construction materials, visible photographic evidence, inference, and unresolved materials. It includes fourteen building groups, including the original four.

## Davy native audit

The v3 native screenshots were inspected against drone272. The frontage count and embedded pane concept are substantially improved, but **v3 is not accepted**. The source includes a large central main-roof cross-gable reaching64.02 m, over a lower covered porch. Its measured ridge runs approximately U110.75–116.48 at V−115.8, with a base about12 m wide. The v3 image only showed the small62.5 m porch. The JSON now includes the relevant source face vertices.

The seven gabled window bays have arched pale heads; the four plain bays have rectangular heads. The north end shows a single arched exit, without the two tall flanking windows added in v3. The roof fascia follows continuous thin slopes, whereas scattered pale blocks in v3 read as disconnected teeth. The forecourt contains grass/turf, chairs and local paving; it should not be entirely pale tile. ## Material appearance samples, inspected after crop correction

[institutional-material-rois.json](../../runtime/research/campus-full-detail-20260905/institutional-material-rois.json) records 56 manually inspected original-pixel rectangles from 17 source images. Each includes the source URL, local file, SHA-256, actual dimensions, surface role, material evidence confidence and lighting condition. Fifty-five samples are accepted at their stated quality; the 36-pixel Kipp sample is rejected for scoring. Davy video samples and thin trim strips remain lower-confidence appearance evidence. Hunt, Alumni House and Gatehouse samples are being researched separately by the terrain/material agent.

All four numbered contact sheets were visually inspected, contaminated first-draft samples were corrected or removed, and the corrected thin limestone crops were inspected individually. Contact-sheet previews are deliberately stretched for inspection; all data rectangles and individual PNGs retain the original source pixels. The source files are not color-calibrated. Warm sun, tree shade, scan color casts, reflected sky and intrinsic mortar/seam shading must remain distinguishable instead of being averaged into one supposedly measured surface color.

The original masterplan page is **2048 × 1582**, and the school Lehrman image is **2200 × 1469**. Earlier masterplan rectangles were mistakenly based on resized tool-preview dimensions; those first crops are superseded. The five corrected context illustrations were inspected, but their full extents must still not be used indiscriminately for color: the limestone illustration contains glass and brick. The separate clean jamb and lintel ROIs exclude them. ## CFTA registration revision 2

Raw USGS LiDAR resolves a freestanding front screen beyond the enclosed roof ring. In the existing 9-degree campus-local U/V axes, its top returns span approximately **U 126.97–193.58 m**, at **H 69.84 m** and nominal **V −171.9 m**. The screen is about 0.5 m deep. Glazing is recessed to approximately **V −174.6 m**, while the enclosed lobby roof is **H 68.54 m**. The pale fascia's photo-interpreted lower edge is approximately **H 66.60 m**. Modeling the roof as the front facade both shortened the frontage and hid the roughly 2.7 m gap between frame and glass. [Raw-point report](../../runtime/research/campus-full-detail-20260905/cfta-lidar-detail-report.json), [point-cloud facade sections](../../runtime/research/campus-full-detail-20260905/cfta-lidar-front-elevations.png), [point-cloud plan](../../runtime/research/campus-full-detail-20260905/cfta-lidar-front-plan.png).

Five independent upper sill clusters lie at U **134.66, 139.52, 144.43, 149.42 and 154.12 m**, all around **H 63.60 m**. They agree with five upper apertures in the county images. Apertures are approximately 2.7 m wide and 3.0 m tall; the five lower glass-block bays align beneath them. The lobby's lower and next floor levels are about **59.90 and 63.60 m**. The main screened curtain opening is approximately **U 158.3–181.7 m**; its glass continues behind the fascia to the roof. Three ground double-door sets center near **U 167.6, 171.0 and 174.9 m** at a provisional threshold **56.65 m**, about 0.35 m above the terrace. [Enlarged window-count grid](../../runtime/research/campus-full-detail-20260905/cfta-west-window-count-grid.png), [revised facade JSON](../../runtime/research/campus-full-detail-20260905/institutional-facades.json).

The front stair runs down eastward along **positive U**, passing landings at approximately **59.85, 58.35, 57.55, 56.85 and 56.30 m**. These are stair-surface returns, not a bare-earth inference. The former negative-V stair direction is superseded. The rounded glass-block drum and precise doorway positions remain photo-derived with explicit tolerances. The registration is suitable for the next native comparison, but it does not certify an exact all-sides match.
