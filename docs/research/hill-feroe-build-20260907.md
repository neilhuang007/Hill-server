# Feroe House construction study — 7 September 2026

This standalone study builds the Head of School Residence, county/Roofer parent `16001511600623C-ba74e78741`, at two blocks per metre. Global coordinates remain X = 2 × east metres, Z = 2 × south metres and Y = 2 × (NAVD88 metres − 25). It does not alter the county source, shared identity registry or neighbouring buildings.

The [August 2019 Bailey Street photograph](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=cODrD_pgaOcm6hJCtBNbhQ&heading=90&pitch=0&fov=70) resolves a cream, smooth rendered-looking residence with an elaborate painted projecting bay. Two storeys of paired sashes, panelled spandrels, continuous pilasters, dentils, a triangular pediment and circular attic light stand above an exposed gray/brown stone lower level. A white-railed raised deck sits at the right and two pale chimney shafts rise above gray overlapping roof units. Trees screen the left bays and entrance. The generic brick interpretation is unsupported by this view.

The original textures are used throughout. Smooth quartz represents the low-frequency warm cream finish, quartz pillars/slabs/stairs represent the painted profiles, tuff bricks provide a coherent muted coursed base, and stone-brick stairs/slabs approximate the medium-gray overlapping roof. These names identify Minecraft proxies, not real stone species or roof products. Flush connected panes represent the sash groups, pale-oak fence geometry provides the two slender painted-column proxies, and light metal bars provide the deck balusters. Photo RGB includes unknown illumination and exposure; it is not treated as intrinsic reflectance.

The 20.3° source minimum-area rectangle is diagonal to the house walls. This study uses a 10.102° frame at XZ (−180, 22) m, agreeing with the 12.63m north wall at 10.105° and the long south run at 9.914°. It retains the exact original footprint, its setbacks and concavities. Every source roof face is checked against the research fingerprint, exact plane coefficients, polygon order and original CityJSON SHA-256.

Dominant accepted faces are 9, 12, 3, 6, 8 and 5, with a maximum source vertex at 67.2192m NAVD88. Tiny fragments 1 and 2 at 73.34–75.25m are explicitly replaced by an extension of neighbouring accepted hip face 6. They are not called chimneys. The photographed west pediment receives a bounded authored gable inside the original plan. Both corrections are written into the study manifest and never presented as surveyed roof geometry.

Low face 4 is interpreted as the raised open deck and low face 11 as a northern terrace. Their exposed surfaces do not become additional occupied storeys. The estimated 56.75m floor follows the observed downhill stone base and 0.5m production DTM; it is not a floor survey. A north entrance has an operable door and a short stair, with another operable door to the deck. Main walls, floor plates and foundations remain physically connected to the measured ground.

One exterior elevation is directly photographed. On the west bay, two paired groups per tier are visibly supported and the tree-hidden third is interpreted. The other three elevations use individually bounded, explicitly labelled interpretations, without copying the ornate front around the house. Their exact openings, the door positions, chimney locations and the deck/terrace details need further exterior references.
