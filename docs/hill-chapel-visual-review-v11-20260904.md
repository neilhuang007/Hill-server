# AI visual review: Hill Alumni Chapel v11

**Decision: pass.** Reviewed on 2026-09-04 by the `campus_reference` AI review agent. This is an AI assessment of the small Alumni Chapel and immediate-grounds sample as a detailed Minecraft replica at **two blocks per metre**. It is not human acceptance, a measured architectural survey, or a claim of photographic equivalence.

The reviewer visually inspected all six native-game PNGs from `runtime/campus-reconstruction/chapel-native-qa/runs/v11-input-isolated-20260904/screenshots/`. The decision rests on their visible architecture and materials. Successful launch, input, palette, and structural checks are separate evidence; their success alone would not establish visual fidelity.

| East elevation and tower | The repeated arcade bays, recessed inner wall and doorway, stone cornice, and upper window rhythm now read coherently. The square tower, higher corner turret, slotted parapet treatment, pale paired belfry panels, and smaller clock preserve the distinguishing silhouette. No further change to placement or height is justified by these unmatched camera views alone. |
| Materials | The visible warm masonry, dressed tan trim, dark roofing, timber, glazing, and metal fixtures form a consistent construction palette. The brownstone and trim textures are interpretations in the dedicated material pack; they are not scanned stone. The veneer pattern remains more regular than the photograph, but this is not a blocker at the review's Minecraft standard. |
| Immediate grounds | Road, entrance paving, steps, planted margins, lawn, and trees give the building a plausible local setting. The images show the approach meeting the building. Stepped grading at this scale and the intentional outer sample cut are accepted. Elevation accuracy and traversability require their separate data and native checks. |

**Remaining evidence-supported visual blockers: none.** The method is suitable to carry into the next bounded building study, with that area's geometry and native views reviewed independently.
