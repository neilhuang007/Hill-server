# Quad and campus layout — native review, 5 September 2026

The Quad revision is suitable for continuing building development in context. **This is not a completed photographic campus replica.** All twelve v6 screenshots were inspected, following ten v5 landscape views and six Athey pane views. Captures use native Minecraft 26.1.2 with original textures.

![Quad at player height](../runtime/campus-reconstruction/chapel-native-qa/runs/campus-context-v6-2x-20260905/screenshots/quad-player-height.png)

The red crosswalk now meets Athey's entrance terrace. The first landscape attempt stopped 13.48 m short; the corrected terrace follows the actual component entrance and floor. Pale perimeter paths surround the open lawn and join Ryan's staircase and garden. Eight tree crowns are interpreted from footage. Their placement, path widths and beds are estimated; grass preserves the measured slope in full blocks.

![Connected window frames](../runtime/campus-reconstruction/chapel-native-qa/runs/campus-context-v6-2x-20260905/screenshots/athey-window-frame-close.png)

Panes lie on the rasterized facade/frame plane. Short pane returns bridge diagonal frame gaps inside the opening, and stair blocks shape arch corners. This removes the prior disconnected sheets. The support audit checks block contact; native close views establish appearance. The facade's original diagonal block steps remain visible at this scale.

No building was moved to close a terrain/path gap. Material selection uses ordinary construction blocks; vanilla mud brick, terracotta and copper preserve different colour families. The Chapel's fine clock face and Hunt's north towers remain coarse. Alumni House's upper front approach is still a stepped dirt study outside this landscape scope.

The [artifact audit](../runtime/campus-history/cleanup-20260915/outputs/runtime/campus-reconstruction/campus-context-v6-2x/artifact-audit.json) independently checks 138,876,727 occupied blocks, 9,216 chunks, component hashes, native pre-upgrade source copies, screenshot hashes and vanilla-only resources. This establishes file/export integrity, not photographic equivalence. Broad peripheral views can contain unloaded distant chunks, so apparent sky gaps in those captures are not evidence of missing source terrain.
