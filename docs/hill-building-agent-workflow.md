# Building reconstruction delegation

The user's September 15 anniversary instruction assigns reference work to **GPT-5.6 Sol, xhigh** and building construction/refinement to **GPT-6 Astra, xhigh** (superseding the September 8 builder effort). Start with Davy, Sherrill Guest House and Quadrivium. Keep new builder contexts small: provide the exact packet, owned files, current artifact and outstanding visual defects instead of the entire research conversation.

Sol owns source searches, image/data collection, building identification, photo registration, measured geometry extraction, palette comparisons and material reasoning. Its packets must distinguish visible observations from inferred controls and unseen elevations. Record source paths/URLs, image hashes, crop coordinates, physical coordinates, roof faces, datum, candidate vanilla textures and the reason for the selected material. A close colour score alone does not establish the real construction material.

The user specifically requests as much detail as the drone fly-through supports. Review consecutive frames and multiple available angles for every active building, using the highest available official footage resolution. Record timestamps and coverage, including roof edges, entrances, sash patterns, railings, support contacts and terrain. Do not treat one convenient still as complete coverage or obscured details as observed facts.

The user also confirms that the covered side passages around the Athey–Dining courtyard must be walk-throughable. Model open bays between attached supports on both sides; preserve their measured roofs and verify actual player clearance along and across the passages. The west passage condition is user-confirmed even where footage obscures its lower enclosure. Include eye-level native views and occupied-shape route checks before acceptance.

Astra owns one building's profile, modeling module, exports and visual refinement. It works from the saved packet, verifies the resulting geometry and compares native screenshots with the supplied references. A modeling contradiction such as a source roof face at ground level beneath a photographed bay must be recorded as a bounded source exception. New evidence or a new material decision goes back to Sol.

The coordinator owns shared helpers, integration and the single native Minecraft capture queue. Builders must not launch competing Minecraft clients or edit each other's modules. Each completed revision is immutable; further refinements go into a new study directory. Keep the existing dirty repository work intact.

All studies use X = 2 × east metres, Z = 2 × south metres and Y = 2 × (NAVD88 metres − 25). Preserve source placement and gaps. Use original vanilla textures and ordinary construction blocks. The user explicitly permits changing glass-pane colours: choose clear or tinted panes to match the photographed glazing, including gray, light gray or black where appropriate, instead of defaulting every window to bright clear glass. Model photographed straight walls as consistent planes at the measured orientation: use only the minimal regular grid steps needed for that rotation, rather than turning roof-raster noise into facade buttresses or zigzags. Curves, bays and projections require source evidence. Window returns remain within the opening/frame, with connected diagonal corners and physically backed heads and sills.

Required building handoff:

- Source packet and hash, owned profile/module, exact study directory and archive hash.
- Roof/footprint changes and uncertainties, explicit visible versus proposed opening schedules, measured grade controls and neighboring gaps.
- Export parity, construction palette, wall closure, pane connections and partial-block backing checks. A connected pane component or zero pair errors alone does not establish enclosure.
- Native cameras followed by a `native-review.json` bound to `archive_sha256` or `sample_blocks_sha256`, with `status`, inspected images, findings and remaining limitations. Use `accepted_for_bounded_integration` only after visual inspection; rejected revisions remain rejected.

The campus preparer refuses an individual study without an accepted review of its exact archive. The assembler checks original world coordinates, overlap, clipping and exported blocks. A bounded building acceptance does not imply photographic completion of unseen faces or acceptance of the entire campus.

For a building-only integration, run `prepare_hill_campus_revision.py --preserve-existing` with the frozen current assembly as `--base` and each accepted new `--study`. This retains every other component, its review and margin, the existing landscape and measured terrain. The separate terrain-refresh mode requires all three of `--terrain`, `--envelopes` and `--developed`; do not use it inadvertently to revert earlier individual refinements. Choose a fresh assembly directory, inspect new native campus views, and only then update `hill-campus-current.json` and the working-state documentation together.

For subsequent building-only assemblies, `assemble_hill_campus_studies.py --reuse-from <accepted-campus-directory>` can preserve unaffected terrain tiles. It requires the accepted manifest and exact artifact audit, identical terrain/frame/landscape, frozen source archives and matching tile hashes. Tiles intersecting either the old or new changed-component extent are rebuilt. Every copied Anvil tile is decoded again before merging, and the normal whole-assembly/native audit still applies. This is an optional preservation path, not a way to bypass visual review.

Before the new campus capture, run `audit_hill_integrated_studies.py <campus-directory> --study <accepted-study>` for each replacement and pass the integration `--camera-config`. It compares complete block states in the study's owned columns from their foundations upward, including air and authored ground, against the combined tile archives. This detects composition changes that can close an accepted window or courtyard route even when the combined archive itself exports correctly. It also checks camera eyes are in air; relocate a standalone observer if a neighboring campus building occupies its position.

Before assembly, independently compare every authored site column and changed ground column with the component's ownership mask, and check that mask against its neighbors. Register exterior walks, landings and steps explicitly in `assembly_site_bounds_uv_m` where needed. Comparing only owned columns cannot detect authored sitework omitted by an undersized mask: rejected campus v11 omitted part of Tuck's sidewalk despite passing that comparison. Tuck v6 corrects the ownership without changing its accepted geometry; the corrected transfer is reviewed in campus v12.

Retained campus landscaping can differ from a study outside its authored scope. Such differences must be investigated, not suppressed: preserve the raw failed report and prove each exact cell equals the previously accepted campus, its study source value is unchanged, and it lies outside the new work and routes. `--retained-site-proof` accepts only that enumerated, assembly-bound proof with unchanged input hashes; any unlisted difference still fails. Campus v12 records this for 176 cells of the existing Athey north Quad approach, separate from the Dining courtyard.
