## People campus

- **Current asset:** Hill School campus v19, reconstructed locally at two blocks per metre from the project's measured terrain, geometry and retained building references. Source attribution and building-review limits remain in the research handoffs and construction records.
- **Source:** The verified `Hill-School-175th-v19-Java-1.21.11.zip`; see the [port handoff](../hill-campus-port-12111.md) and [campus template guide](../CAMPUS_TEMPLATE.md).
- **Distribution:** The immutable `hill_people_template_campus_v19_2x.tgz` is staged separately from Git. Its checksum and counts are pinned in [`campus-template.env`](../../server-assets/campus-template.env). All 25 overworld region files are copied unchanged into the package; no player data or extra dimensions are included.
- **Use:** New private People entries and owner-confirmed resets. Existing participant worlds are preserved. The plugin fails closed if the required template is missing.

Journey and Place worlds are generated by the Hill175 plugin. Earlier People deployments used the school-authorized LiDAR `structure.nbt`, followed by the one-block-per-metre VoxelEarth/Roofer v15 template. Those historical assets, attribution records and existing private worlds remain separate from the current v19 template. `server-assets/worlds.yml` distinguishes the current `people-campus` provenance from `historical-people-campus-v15`; the installer reads executable pins from `campus-template.env`.
