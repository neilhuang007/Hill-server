# Sharing the campus

We plan to share the digital Hill campus through a private website. Alumni will be able to revisit familiar places, while students can take a copy of the map and make it their own.

## Exploring

Alumni can explore the campus directly in their browser, without installing apps, downloading files or owning Minecraft. They can simply open the website, enter the password and look around.

Students who want to build or experiment can download the latest campus map from the same website. They will need Minecraft Java Edition to open it. Anything they change will stay in their own copy and will not affect the school's shared campus.

## Keeping things current

The map available to view and download will be updated every five days. Downloaded copies will stay as they are, so students can return to the website whenever they want a newer version.

The school's shared campus will also be backed up every five minutes, allowing staff to recover earlier work if something is accidentally changed or damaged.

## Access and privacy

For safety, the website will be password protected and closed to the public, with search engine indexing blocked. Please keep the website link, password and any downloaded copies within the intended Hill community.
