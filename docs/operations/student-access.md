**TLDR:** Choose **Explore campus** to look around in your browser, or **Download map** for your own Minecraft Java world. To build with other students, use the server address and joining instructions from the organizer. The current demo has its own password. Hill Microsoft access will come after school setup and testing.

You do not need to jump straight into Minecraft to see what is taking shape. The campus website lets you explore the buildings and grounds in a 3D browser tour, with no Minecraft account or server login required. The website is currently a local preview.

If you would like a campus of your own to wander through, choose **Download map**. This is a world for Minecraft Java Edition, so you will need that edition and a compatible game version to open it. Changes in your downloaded copy stay there, separate from the shared campus.

For the shared experience, ask the organizer for the server address and the right joining instructions for your Minecraft edition. In the current demo, use an empty hand on the lobby floor to open **Competition Sign In**. Choose **Register** on your first visit and **Sign In** when you return. Choose a fresh password just for this server. The demo accepts development accounts.

When Hill enables Microsoft access, open the school link in your browser. Return to the game, open **School Sign In**, and choose **Enter Browser Code**. If you get stuck, send the organizer the error message and your Minecraft edition. Keep passwords and verification codes private.
